**Water Ford** adds terrain tiles for Factorio 2.1: a **ford you can walk
across**, the matching **Wooden Flooring** for dry land — and the
**Cobblestone** pair: a **road ford vehicles actually drive across**, plus
the **Cobblestone Path** for dry land. The wooden pair keeps footpaths over
the water; the cobble pair opens it to traffic.

## Wooden Ford

- **Cross water on foot** — ford rivers, lakes and your Cargo Ships
  waterways. Biters and Pyanodon's land caravans wade it too.
- **Cargo Ships vessels still sail over it** and fish swim across the deck:
  the crossing keeps the water navigable.
- **Vehicles keep out**: cars and tanks are stopped at the shore, so a ford
  stays a footpath. For drivable crossings, that's the Cobblestone Ford
  below.
- The deck lies **under animated water**, with wooden pilings standing
  semi-submerged at the rims and ballast stones tinting at the waterline:
  a real shallow crossing, not a floating walkway.
- Water stays water: placeable on water only, and fish and other
  water-dwellers treat it as water. Offshore pumps can no longer be placed
  on it.
- Unbuildable: a ford is a crossing, not landfill — you can't construct
  buildings on it.

## Wooden Flooring

- Honey-toned plank boards for camps, yards and walkways — **land only**.
- A raised **wooden bumper edge** at the waterline and a wooden **trim**
  against other terrain, so board edges read as built, not cut.
- Same boards, same palette as the ford deck — the two tiles read as one
  family.

## Cobblestone Ford

- **Drive the crossing**: cars and tanks roll straight over, so the
  outposts on the far bank are a road trip, not a bridge-building project.
- **Walkers stop at the shore**: you and your enemies alike. The road is
  yours — biters can't use it to walk into your base.
- **Hand-laid cobbles** rise through the water: crowns breach the surface,
  the joints below read submerged — a road that wades, not a floating
  platform.
- **Ships still sail over it**, and the deck stays water to everything
  else: no offshore pumps, no buildings, placeable on water only.

## Cobblestone Path

- **Pave roads and yards** in grey-blue cobbles that read as one family
  with the ford's deck.
- **Land only**: paving won't go over water — for a crossing, use the
  Cobblestone Ford.

Works standalone; with Pyanodon's Alien Life all four tiles join the
wood/stone chains and the caravans get their crossings.
