**Water Ford** adds terrain tiles for Factorio 2.1: a **ford you can walk
across**, the matching **Wooden Flooring** for dry land — and, from 1.1.0,
the **Cobblestone** pair: a **road ford vehicles actually drive across**,
plus the **Cobblestone Path** for dry land.

## Wooden Ford

- Cross water on foot — ford across rivers, lakes and your Cargo Ships waterways.
- Cross water on foot — ford across rivers, lakes and your Cargo Ships waterways.
- **Every walker crosses**: you, biters, and Pyanodon's land caravans — while fish swim across the deck and **Cargo Ships vessels still sail over it**.
- **Vehicles keep out**: cars and tanks are stopped at the shore — the ford is a footpath, not a road (a drivable road ford is planned).
- The deck lies **under animated water**, with wooden pilings standing semi-submerged at the rims and ballast stones tinting at the waterline: a real shallow crossing, not a floating walkway.
- Water stays water: the ford is placeable on water only, and fish and other water-dwellers treat it as water. Offshore pumps can no longer be placed on it.
- Unbuildable: you can't construct buildings on fords as they are not intended as a substitute for landfill.

## Wooden Flooring

- Honey-toned plank boards for camps, yards and walkways — **land only**.
- A raised **wooden bumper edge** at the waterline and a wooden **trim** against other terrain, so board edges read as built, not cut.
- Same boards, same palette as the ford deck — the two tiles read as one family.

## Cobblestone Ford (1.1.0)

- The **road crossing**: vehicles drive across while players, land caravans
  and enemies are stopped at the shore — the feet/wheels divide, built from
  the same three collision layers as the wooden ford (nothing you place can
  be run over).
- **Hand-laid cobblestones** rise through the water — some crowns breach
  the surface, joints stay dark, everything below reads submerged. 16
  authored variants per size (1×1, 2×2, 4×4).
- Ships still sail over it; offshore pumps are refused; placeable on water
  only. Unbuildable, like the wooden ford.
- Recipe: **1 Cobblestone Path + 100 stone** (10 sand + 1 stone brick for
  the path; with Pyanodon's Alien Life the ford consumes 1 path + 200
  gravel). Unlocks with the existing water-ford technology.

## Cobblestone Path (1.1.0)

- The **land partner**: grey-blue cobbles for yards and roads, reading as
  one family with the ford's deck.
- Slightly faster than bare land (1.1 walking speed; the cobble ford runs
  0.9, the wooden footpath 0.8-band).
- Recipe: **2 stone + 1 stone brick**.

Works standalone; with Pyanodon's Alien Life all four tiles join the wood/
stone chains and the caravans get their crossings.
