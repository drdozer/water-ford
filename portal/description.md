**Water Ford** is a small mod adding a single tile: a ford you can walk across.

## What it does

- A walkable ford that can only be placed **on water** — build ford strips across rivers, lakes and your Cargo Ships waterways.
- The deck sits **under animated water**, with wooden pilings standing semi-submerged at the rims: the visual is a real shallow crossing, not a floating walkway.
- The ford keeps its water collision, so **Cargo Ships vessels still sail over it**, and **offshore pumps and other water-dwellers treat it as water** — river infrastructure works on fords.
- **Land caravans from Pyanodon's Alien Life can walk over it**, which is the mod's whole point. Both mods are optional dependencies; everything here works standalone.

## Crafting

One ford per craft, 10 s. Fords are deliberately slow and bulky — they're accent pieces to be used sparingly, not filler:

|   | Ingredients | Unlocked by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vanilla | 4× wood, 1× iron-stick, 50× stone | Concrete |
| With Pyanodon's Alien Life | 2× treated-wood, 1× iron-stick, 8× bolts, 1× log, 200× gravel (bed ballast) | Creosote |

## Not a substitute for landfill

- You can't over-build: won't allow you to place buildings on it, or cover it in other flooring.
- Only on water: refuses placement on land.
- It's a crossing, not terrain creation.
