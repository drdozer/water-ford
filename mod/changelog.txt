Version: 1.0.1 (unreleased, dev tiles)
Date: 17. 10. 2026
  Changes:
    - Wooden Boards gets a wooden bumper-board edge where it meets water
      (replaces the inherited concrete stone curb).
    - Wooden Boards meets other terrain with a wooden trim instead of the
      inherited stone corner and side pieces.
    - Grass-water-ford corners beside Wooden Boards render as wooden
      three-way transitions (were stone).
    - Wooden Ford deck: pebbles and small stones sprinkle the boards
      as ballast.
    - Proper item and technology icons.
    - Rewritten item, tile and technology descriptions: short tooltips,
      expanded text on the Factoriopedia tile pages.
    - Promotion pass: the real water-ford tile is unfrozen onto the boards-ford
      ladder (16/16/16 variants at 1x1/2x2/4x4; C-depth 128px sprites at 0.25
      scale, probabilities 1 on the big sizes).
    - Land family collapsed to one tileset: boards-land (was the
      boards-land-a/b/c compare set), with item and recipe.
    - Boards recipe redesign, both tiles under the water-ford technology:
      floor boards 2 wood + 1 iron-stick -> 1 (py: 2 treated-wood +
      1 iron-stick + 8 bolts -> 1). Ford boards are composed: vanilla
      1 floor board + 2 wood + 100 stone -> 1 ford; py 1 floor board +
      1 log + 200 gravel -> 1 ford. One board per craft, scarce by design.
    - Retired the dev compare tiles, items, recipes, locale entries and stale
      A/B sheets; planks.png deck art demoted from the live tile.
    - Six map-editor-only compare tiles: boards-land-a/b/c (wooden boards on
      land) and boards-ford-a/b/c (the boards as ford deck under the animated
      water veil). Three sprite depths each (A 32px @1.0, B 64px @0.5,
      C 128px @0.25 - all render 32 world px) for picking the sprite basis.
      Locked look: Blender boards, ring-grain rf~8 + pores + deep winter line.
      No items, recipes, or terrain autoplace.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 13. 10. 2026
  Changes:
    - Water Ford: a walkable, place-on-water tile - the deck sits under animated
      water with wooden pilings semi-submerged at the rims; ships still cross.
    - Without Pyanodon Alien Life: unlocked by the concrete tech, crafted from
      4 wood, 1 iron stick, 50 stone.
    - With Pyanodon Alien Life: unlocked after creosote, crafted from 2
      treated-wood, 1 iron-stick, 8 bolts, 1 log, 200 gravel (bed ballast).
    - Recipes craft 1 ford per 10s; fords are intentionally scarce.
    - Fords can only be placed on water (enforced at runtime; illegal
      placements are reverted and refunded).
